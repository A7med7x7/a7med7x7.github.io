I feel bad whenever I think about this part of my life, and I'm trying to put it down for my friends. I love my friends, all of them, with no exception, and I love them equally. unlike many, I can't pretend to love people, I can pretend that I like them. those I don't, I don't. but the ones I mean, I do love them. and like anyone else, they have shadows they don't admit in public: fears, doubts, intrusive thoughts, self destructive patterns. that is part of being human, and I don't see it as any different from a physical illness.

I [once](https://a7med7x7.github.io/posts/before-you-heal-someone/) made helping people the priority of my life. it led me to realize that even if you hand someone the medicine, many will refuse to take it. fast forward more than five years, and for once I felt I had climbed over a huge obstacle in the way I perceive life. as if a fifth dimension had been unveiled to me, a dimension of awareness, of self awareness, that made me conscious of everything around me. I could see clearly. sometimes too clearly to enjoy life.

And at the crest, happy with this discovery and not knowing what waited after it, I set out to help those around me, because now I could see more of what drags them down. to be fair, some attempts were successful. others were painfully not. I wanted my people to end the wretchedness they live in. little did I know it wasn't that easy. I'm not the one fighting the demons, they are.

People are not getting better, even when everything around them seems set perfectly, and it is the right time, where [conditions are favorable](https://a7med7x7.github.io/posts/good-times-for-a-change/). they still cling to their chaos comfort. that part I understand. but getting out still takes courage, and it still [takes action](https://a7med7x7.github.io/posts/about-the-way-forward/). I don't see my friends doing the work it takes to be better, to feel better. I try to help them all the time, but they don't help themselves, and they are not really good at getting out of these holes. as I write this I realize it isn't only the inner introspection they skip. some of them are also falling behind in preparing for their careers, even though I'm there for them, trying my best. at any part where they need help.

What I remember is that whatever I passed, I passed by myself. I worked hard, and got knocked down and beaten to get there. and when I finally had the chance to get better, with someone offering me help, I listened carefully. I had to swallow bitter words about myself and about the reality of the world. I had to isolate myself so I wouldn't hurt people with my inconsistent, self destructive patterns, and to learn what I needed to learn before doing any other work in life. I used the advice they gave me well. I reported my progress, and this blog holds tens of pieces of evidence of that: days I spent reading about psychological disorders, anxiety, attachment.

But they don't seem to use any of these things well, and I hate it. I don't blame them. I just feel like the whole environment around me keeps taking more of my energy without ever progressing, without ever helping me even feel happy more often of my efforts to save people, or to learn anything new . it also makes me lonely. I lack the intellectual companionship I need, people who make me think harder, and I don't like that. I want company. I want to help people climb the mountain too, not just tell my friends what the view looks like from up there.

I feel the rate at which I'm moving in life is very different from my friends', and I was doing my best to close this divergence, to shrink it. I really don't want to let go of my friends. but I feel consumed. people think I got it easier in life, while I have too much to deal with myself. and on top of that comes this sudden period where all my friends are going through hard times at once, and I feel like I'm holding all of them at the same time. it is beyond my comprehension.

Listening to all their suffering makes me sick too. I might need some time for myself. this is not to dismiss that if someone wants help, I would offer all I have. but I can't keep following up with everyone, not again. not now.
