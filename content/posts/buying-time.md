People waste so much time, and not on one thing only. it is spread across many small, tiny things they do throughout their day. if they cut hours, minutes, even seconds, by automating a task or making a chore faster, or whatever else, they would reach this concept of buying time. you get the minutes back, and with them a sense of having enough time, which researchers call time affluence. and I'm going to show you examples of how buying time can actually help in the process of feeling more, reflecting, having me time, time for entertainment, and enough time so you won't be late to your lectures.

## Clothing

This one is tricky, but it is important. simple and easy to wear fashion packed my teenage years, and I only wore full suits and jackets for weddings. that changed when I turned 20, but not so much. I was selecting things without knowing why, but they were all easy to wear and comfortable for sitting long hours at the desk, whether for uni or work.

I bought clothes of the same color (black is my favorite for t shirts), so I wouldn't have analysis paralysis every time I decided what to put on, a personal uniform of sorts. I didn't have a name for what I was avoiding back then. it was decision fatigue, the idea that every small choice draws from the same limited pool of attention. the research on it is mixed, but I know the feeling. my jackets were hung in the room so I could just wear them, and my pants almost all had an elastic waistband or none at all. I never buy pants that need a belt.

That extends to even the type of fabric and how it feels. I don't pick clothes that need ironing every time I wash them. that's about saving time, not laziness. this extends to my shoes too. my favorite type is slip on shoes, the ones that come with no laces, and I still love them to this very day. I have around 13 different pairs now, but at least 3 of them are slip on, and those are the ones I wear for almost all my outings, like meeting friends at a park, walking, or going to the mosque.

Now that saves me an enormous amount of time. I can spend that time checking on friends to see if they are out, or on taking a shower before going out, almost anywhere. and I would recommend this to anyone who is at their university or high school. you don't need to look rich and fancy, or wear old money styles and full suits that would take you about an hour to prepare. easy, plug and that's it.
